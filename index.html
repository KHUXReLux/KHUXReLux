How to play

KHUXReLux needs game data that is not ours to hand out, so there is no public installer and this site hosts no game files.

This is a work in progress: expect bugs, crashes and screens that are not there yet.

What a setup needs

  • An Android device with an arm64 processor and about 25 GB free (the high-resolution data alone is about 8 GB)
  • The KHUXReLux app and a data package from the project
  • Nothing else: the server and your save live on the device

Questions

Do I need a PC?

Not to play. The PC is only used by the project to prepare the data packages.

Is it online?

No. The server runs inside the app and your save stays on the device. There are no other players: Union Cross, parties and rankings are local.

Can I download it?

Not yet, and not from this page. Follow the changelog and the status to see how it moves.

Found a bug?

Tell us on the KHUXReLux Discord server (see the Server page): what you did step by step, what happened instead, the screen or quest number, and a screenshot or video if you can.

How you can help

What helps most is knowledge: how screens, events and modes looked and worked. A few Union Cross events and Colosseum months had to be rebuilt without their maps. We ask only for information, never for game files.

What we are looking for

  • MOST WANTEDThe maps and bosses of eight Union Cross events from 2019-2020: Faline Snuggly, Baymax Snuggly, Rapunzel Spirit Parts, Boo Snuggly, Cream Soda, Union Cross 2 (Oct 2019), New Year's Chirithy Hat and Union Cross 14 (Sep-Oct 2020). Screenshots or videos of the stages are enough
  • EVENTSRound-by-round lineups of the 16 Colosseum months that were recreated from the wiki, to check the enemies, the waves and their order
  • KNOWLEDGEHow PvP worked: points per match, how classes were decided, what the rewards were, how matches felt. Our rules there are our own design
  • ANYTHINGYour own screenshots and videos of any screen of the game, any year

Please do not send or post game files (APK, OBB, downloaded data, backups): we cannot accept them.

To get in touch, see the Server page.

Our neighbours

This restoration stands on the work of people who preserved and documented the game long before us.

  • KxosRe-Union X, a sibling restoration with its own server. Its code is MIT licensed, its notes are a constant reference and the two projects share research, notably on the Colosseum and Union Cross. The look and structure of this page follow the Re-Union X portal.
  • thethinyKHUxTools and the other open tools for reading the game's files, which everything else here stands on.
  • RoboloidThe medal voice files, collected on the Roboloid KHUχ site.
  • KHUX Wikikhuxwiki.com: enemy lineups, event calendars and board data. It is how the Colosseum seasons and Union Cross stages with no surviving maps were rebuilt.
  • CommunityEveryone who still remembers the quests, the events and the numbers and who shared them: screenshots, videos and corrections are what close the gaps.
